# 「原形が続く表現」イラスト制作記録

組み込みの ImageGen で、カード画面に表示するイラストシートを7枚作成。保存先は `public/flashcard-images/direct-atlas-1.webp` から `direct-atlas-7.webp`。

## シート 1

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: ONE illustration sprite atlas for an English-learning app. A landscape 4:3 canvas with EXACTLY NINE independent illustrations in a perfectly regular 3-column by 3-row grid. Each tile occupies exactly one third of the width and height. Grid divisions precisely at 33.333% and 66.667% in both axes. No gutters, no borders, no margins; scenes can abut at grid lines. Keep people and key objects centered within each tile, well away from tile edges. Style: warm, polished, softly painted 2D editorial storybook illustration with clear actions, soft ivory, terracotta and teal accents. Distinct settings for each tile. No text, letters, numbers, speech bubbles, watermarks, labels or logos.
top left: A woman heading to a takeout counter to grab a paper bag of food.
top center: A man stepping outside toward a mailbox on a post.
top right: An adult and a child looking for the child's mother among people at a park.
middle left: Someone points toward an open garage with shelves while another walks toward it.
middle center: A shopper reaching for a milk carton in a small grocery store.
middle right: Two sweaty runners walking toward a drinking fountain.
bottom left: Two fans walking toward a sports field where a soccer match is visible.
bottom center: A woman walking toward a man sitting alone on a park bench to talk.
bottom right: One traveler walks across a train station concourse to greet a waving friend.
